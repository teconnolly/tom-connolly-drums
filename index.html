    04 The work

    Lessons for the long build. Consultations for the stuck point.

    Lesson · 45 min

    Weekly playing, built on purpose.

    Beginner through advanced. The plan follows where you actually are: time, limb independence, reading, styles, and a practice you will still do on a Wednesday night.

    • A repeating weekly slot, if you want the rhythm of showing up
    • Hands, feet, and the ear that tells them what to do
    • Charts, grooves, and the quiet work between lessons
    See lesson times

    Consultation · 60 min

    An outside ear for a real problem.

    For working musicians, teachers, and bands. Bring the thing that has stopped moving: a technical ceiling, a chart you do not trust, a practice week that produces nothing, a gig you are walking into tight.

    • Diagnosis first, then a smaller plan than you expect
    • Touch, setup, reading, and pre-gig focus
    • You leave with one focus for the next stretch of work
    See consultation times
